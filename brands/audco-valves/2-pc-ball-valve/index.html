Audco 2 PC Ball Valve refers to a type of ball valve manufactured by Audco, a well-known industrial valve manufacturer. In this context, “2 PC” stands for two-piece design, indicating that the valve body is constructed from two separate pieces that are bolted or threaded together.

This design facilitates easier maintenance and repair compared to one-piece designs while maintaining strong durability and reliable sealing performance in demanding industrial environments.

Here are some common features and characteristics associated with Audco 2 PC Ball Valves:

Key Features & Characteristics

  • Two-Piece Construction: Valve body composed of two separate pieces allowing easier access for maintenance.
  • Corrosion Resistant Materials: Often manufactured using stainless steel or cast steel for durability.
  • Full Port or Reduced Port Options: Available depending on application and required flow characteristics.
  • Fire Safe Design: Maintains integrity and performance even under high temperature conditions.
  • Blow-Out Proof Stem: Prevents the valve stem from being expelled under pressure.
  • Various End Connections: Threaded, flanged, or welded connections available for different piping systems.
  • Multiple Seat and Seal Options: Allows compatibility with different fluids and operating conditions.
  • API Compliance: Manufactured according to industry standards such as American Petroleum Institute (API).
