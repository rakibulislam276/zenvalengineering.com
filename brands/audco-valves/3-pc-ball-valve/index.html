Audco 3 PC Ball Valve refers to a type of ball valve manufactured by Audco, a renowned company in the field of industrial valves. The term “3 PC” stands for three-piece construction, meaning the valve body is assembled from three separate components: two end caps and a central body section.

This design allows easy maintenance, inspection, and cleaning of internal components without removing the entire valve from the pipeline, making it ideal for demanding industrial applications.

Here are some common features and characteristics associated with Audco 3 PC Ball Valves:

Key Features & Characteristics

  • Three-Piece Construction: Valve body consists of three sections bolted together for easy maintenance and disassembly.
  • Stainless Steel Construction: Provides corrosion resistance, durability, and suitability for multiple industrial applications.
  • Full Port or Reduced Port Options: Available depending on application and flow requirements.
  • Fire Safe Design: Ensures valve integrity even in high temperature or fire conditions.
  • Blow-Out Proof Stem: Prevents stem ejection under high pressure.
  • Various End Connections: Threaded, flanged, and welded options available to suit different piping systems.
  • Multiple Seat and Seal Options: Compatible with various fluids, temperatures, and operating environments.
  • API Certification: Manufactured according to recognized industrial standards such as American Petroleum Institute (API).
