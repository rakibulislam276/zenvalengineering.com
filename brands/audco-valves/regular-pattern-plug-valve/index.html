Audco Regular Pattern Plug Valve

Audco Regular Pattern Plug Valve, Cast Iron Plug Valve Dealer and Supplier in India.

Audco Regular Pattern Plug Valve is a quarter-turn rotary valve used to control the flow of fluids in pipelines. The valve features a cylindrical or tapered plug with a port (opening) that allows fluid to pass when aligned with the pipeline.

Plug valves are known for their simple design, reliable sealing performance, and fast operation. They are widely used in industrial applications requiring quick shut-off or flow regulation.

Key Features & Characteristics

  • Plug Design: Cylindrical or tapered plug rotates inside the valve body to control fluid flow.
  • Quarter Turn Operation: Valve opens or closes with a simple 90-degree rotation.
  • Durable Materials: Manufactured using cast iron, ductile iron, carbon steel, stainless steel, or alloy materials depending on application requirements.
  • Multiple End Connections: Available with flanged, threaded, or welded connections.
  • Various Pressure Ratings: Suitable for different industrial pressure classes including Class 150, 300, 600, and higher.
  • Wide Size Range: Available in multiple sizes to suit different pipe diameters and flow conditions.
  • Reliable Flow Control: Provides effective shut-off and throttling performance.
  • Industrial Versatility: Suitable for liquid, gas, and slurry handling applications.
