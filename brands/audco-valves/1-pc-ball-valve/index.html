Audco 1 PC Ball Valve is a premium industrial valve known for its robust single-piece body construction. The 1 PC (One Piece) design means the valve body is made from a single cast material which enhances durability and reduces potential leak paths.

This compact and reliable ball valve is widely used in oil & gas, petrochemical, water treatment, and industrial flow control systems.

  • One-Piece Design: Strong single body construction.
  • Reduced Leak Points: Minimal joints reduce leakage risk.
  • Cast Steel Construction: Corrosion resistant and durable.
  • Full / Reduced Port Options: Multiple configurations available.
  • Fire Safe Design: Operates reliably in high temperatures.
