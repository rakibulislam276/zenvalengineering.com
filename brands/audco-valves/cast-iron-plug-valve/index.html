Audco Cast Iron Plug Valve

Audco Cast Iron Plug Valve, Cast Steel Plug Valve Dealer and Supplier in India.

Audco Cast Iron Plug Valves are designed for reliable flow control in pipelines using cast iron as the primary construction material. Cast iron provides durability, corrosion resistance, and strength, making these valves suitable for many industrial applications.

Plug valves operate using a cylindrical or tapered plug with a port that rotates within the valve body to regulate fluid flow. These valves are commonly used where quick shut-off and simple operation are required.

Key Features & Characteristics

  • Cast Iron Construction: Provides durability and resistance to corrosion and wear.
  • Plug Valve Design: Cylindrical or tapered plug rotates to control flow through the pipeline.
  • Quarter Turn Operation: Valve opens or closes with a 90-degree rotation.
  • Multiple End Connections: Available with flanged, threaded, or socket-weld connections.
  • Pressure Ratings: Available in pressure classes such as Class 125 and Class 250.
  • Wide Size Range: Available in multiple valve sizes to suit different pipeline diameters.
  • Reliable Flow Isolation: Suitable for on/off flow control applications.
  • Industrial Applications: Designed for water supply systems, wastewater treatment plants, and general industrial use.
