Audco 3PC Ball Valve Flanged

Audco 3PC Ball Valve Flanged and Manufacturer of Audco Screwed or Socket Weld Ball Valve.

Audco 3PC Ball Valve Flanged refers to a specific type of three-piece ball valve manufactured by Audco that features flanged ends for connection to pipelines. Flanged ball valves are widely used in industrial applications because they provide a strong, secure, and leak-resistant connection.

These valves are known for their durability, ease of maintenance, and reliable performance in demanding operating conditions across various industries.

Key Features & Characteristics

  • Three-Piece Construction: Valve body consists of three components allowing easy maintenance and cleaning without removing the valve from the pipeline.
  • Flanged Ends: Flanged connections provide strong and leak-resistant pipeline connections and allow easy integration into industrial piping systems.
  • Stainless Steel Construction: High-quality stainless steel ensures corrosion resistance and long service life.
  • Full Port or Reduced Port: Available depending on the application and required flow capacity.
  • Fire-Safe Design: Maintains valve integrity and functionality even in high temperature or fire conditions.
  • Blow-Out Proof Stem: Prevents stem ejection under high pressure conditions.
  • API Certification: Manufactured according to industry standards such as the American Petroleum Institute (API).
