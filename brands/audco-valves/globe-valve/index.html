Audco Globe Valve

Audco Globe Valve is a high-quality industrial valve designed for precise flow control in pipelines. Globe valves are commonly used to regulate or completely stop the flow of liquids or gases in various industrial systems.

The name “globe valve” comes from the globe-shaped body design that houses the internal components responsible for controlling flow through the valve.

Audco globe valves are widely used in industries requiring accurate throttling and reliable shut-off performance.

Key Features & Characteristics

  • Body Construction: Globe-shaped valve body with a movable plug (disc) that regulates flow.
  • High Quality Materials: Manufactured using stainless steel, carbon steel, or alloy steel depending on the application.
  • Bonnet Design: May feature bolted bonnet or pressure seal bonnet for high pressure applications.
  • Precise Flow Control: Designed specifically for throttling and regulating fluid flow.
  • Different Valve Types: Includes angle globe valves and Y-pattern globe valves.
  • Various End Connections: Flanged, threaded, or welded ends to suit industrial piping systems.
  • Multiple Pressure Ratings: Available in various pressure classes for different industrial conditions.
  • Actuation Options: Manual handwheel operation or automated actuation using pneumatic or electric actuators.
  • Seal Types: Soft seats or metal-to-metal seals for high temperature and high pressure service.
