Uni Klinger Piston Valve

Uni Klinger Piston Valves are designed for reliable and efficient flow control in demanding industrial applications. These valves utilize a piston-based sealing mechanism that ensures tight shut-off, minimal leakage, and long service life.

Widely used in steam and fluid handling systems, Uni Klinger piston valves offer precise control and excellent durability even under high pressure and temperature conditions.

Key Features
  • High performance piston mechanism for accurate flow control
  • Excellent tight shut-off with minimal leakage
  • Linear motion ensures smooth operation
  • Durable construction for long service life
  • Suitable for high pressure and temperature applications
  • Low maintenance and easy servicing
  • Reliable performance in industrial environments

Applications

  • Steam distribution systems
  • Power plants
  • Chemical and process industries
  • Petrochemical plants
  • Oil & gas industries
  • Boiler and condensate systems
