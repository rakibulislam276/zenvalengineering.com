Uni Klinger Thermostatic Steam Traps

Uni Klinger Thermostatic Steam Traps are designed to automatically discharge condensate based on temperature differences between steam and condensate. These traps ensure efficient removal of condensate while preventing the loss of live steam, making them highly energy-efficient and reliable.

Ideal for applications requiring precise temperature control, these steam traps provide smooth operation, reduced energy loss, and consistent performance across various industrial processes.

Key Features
  • Operates based on temperature difference between steam and condensate
  • Ensures efficient condensate discharge with minimal steam loss
  • Excellent energy-saving performance
  • Provides accurate and responsive operation
  • Suitable for varying load conditions
  • Durable construction for long service life
  • Low maintenance and reliable performance

Applications

  • Steam tracing systems
  • Heat exchangers
  • Process heating applications
  • Chemical and pharmaceutical industries
  • Food processing industries
  • Industrial steam systems
