Uni Klinger Inverted Bucket Traps (UIBT)

Uni Klinger Inverted Bucket Traps (UIBT) are designed for efficient and reliable removal of condensate from steam systems while preventing the loss of live steam. These traps operate on the principle of buoyancy, ensuring continuous and controlled discharge.

Known for their rugged construction and dependable performance, inverted bucket traps are widely used in industrial applications where durability and efficiency are essential.

Key Features
  • Operates on buoyancy principle for effective condensate removal
  • Reliable bucket mechanism for automatic operation
  • Continuous and controlled discharge of condensate
  • Automatic air venting for improved system efficiency
  • Robust and durable construction
  • Suitable for high pressure steam systems
  • Easy maintenance and long service life

Applications

  • Steam distribution systems
  • Process industries
  • Power plants
  • Chemical and petrochemical industries
  • Heating systems
  • Industrial steam applications
