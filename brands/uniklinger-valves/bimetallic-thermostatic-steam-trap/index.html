Uni Klinger Bimetallic Thermostatic Steam Trap

Uni Klinger Bimetallic Thermostatic Steam Traps are designed to efficiently remove condensate from steam systems based on temperature differences. These traps utilize a bimetallic element that responds to changes in temperature, ensuring controlled discharge and improved energy efficiency.

Ideal for applications requiring precise temperature-based operation, these traps offer durability, reliability, and consistent performance in demanding industrial environments.

Key Features
  • Bimetallic element for temperature-sensitive operation
  • Efficient condensate discharge with minimal steam loss
  • Modulating discharge for varying load conditions
  • Compact and easy-to-install design
  • Robust construction for long service life
  • Automatic air venting capability
  • Low maintenance and reliable performance

Applications

  • Steam tracing systems
  • Heat exchangers
  • Process industries
  • Chemical and pharmaceutical industries
  • Power plants
  • Industrial steam applications
