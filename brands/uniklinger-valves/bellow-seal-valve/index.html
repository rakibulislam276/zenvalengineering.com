Uni Klinger Bellow Seal Valve

Uni Klinger Bellow Seal Valves are specially designed to provide leak-proof and reliable sealing performance in critical industrial applications. These valves use a bellows sealing mechanism that prevents leakage and protects the environment from fugitive emissions.

Ideal for handling hazardous and high-value fluids, these valves ensure safe operation and long-term reliability in demanding process conditions.

Key Features
  • Advanced bellow seal design for zero leakage
  • Prevents fugitive emissions and ensures safety
  • Maintenance-free sealing system
  • Excellent corrosion resistance
  • Long service life in harsh environments
  • Suitable for critical and hazardous applications
  • Complies with industrial standards

Applications

  • Chemical processing industries
  • Petrochemical plants
  • Pharmaceutical industries
  • Oil & gas sector
  • Steam and fluid systems
  • Hazardous fluid handling applications
