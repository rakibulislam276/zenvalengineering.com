Uni Klinger Thermodynamic Steam Traps

Uni Klinger Thermodynamic Steam Traps are designed for efficient removal of condensate from steam systems while preventing the loss of live steam. These traps operate based on thermodynamic principles, ensuring reliable and energy-efficient performance.

Known for their compact design and durability, these steam traps are widely used in industrial applications where robust and low-maintenance solutions are required.

Key Features
  • Operates based on thermodynamic principles
  • Efficient condensate removal with minimal steam loss
  • Disc mechanism for automatic opening and closing
  • Compact and simple design
  • Highly durable and robust construction
  • Suitable for high pressure steam systems
  • Low maintenance and long operational life

Applications

  • Steam distribution systems
  • Heating and process industries
  • Power plants
  • Chemical and petrochemical industries
  • Boiler and condensate systems
  • Industrial steam applications
