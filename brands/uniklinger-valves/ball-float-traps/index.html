Uni Klinger Ball Float Traps

Uni Klinger Ball Float Traps are designed for continuous and efficient removal of condensate from steam systems while preventing the loss of live steam. These traps operate using a float mechanism that responds to condensate levels, ensuring reliable and automatic discharge.

Known for their smooth operation and high efficiency, ball float traps are ideal for applications where consistent condensate removal and energy efficiency are critical.

Key Features
  • Ball float mechanism for automatic condensate discharge
  • Continuous operation without steam loss
  • Efficient handling of varying condensate loads
  • Integrated air venting for improved performance
  • Durable construction for high pressure applications
  • Corrosion resistant materials
  • Easy maintenance and long service life

Applications

  • Heat exchangers
  • Steam heating systems
  • Process industries
  • Power plants
  • Chemical and petrochemical industries
  • Boiler condensate systems
