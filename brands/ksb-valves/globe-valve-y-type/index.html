KSB Globe Valve Y Type

KSB Globe Valve Y Type is designed for efficient flow regulation with reduced pressure drop compared to conventional globe valves. The Y-type body design allows smoother fluid flow, making it ideal for applications where minimizing flow resistance is important.

These valves provide precise control over fluid flow while maintaining high performance in high pressure and temperature conditions, making them suitable for demanding industrial environments.

Key Features
  • Y-type design for reduced pressure drop
  • Provides precise flow control and throttling
  • Improved flow efficiency compared to standard globe valves
  • Robust construction for high pressure applications
  • Rising or non-rising stem configurations
  • Flanged or butt weld end connections
  • Long service life with minimal maintenance

Applications

  • Oil and gas industries
  • Petrochemical plants
  • Power generation units
  • Chemical processing industries
  • Steam and fluid control systems
  • Industrial process pipelines
