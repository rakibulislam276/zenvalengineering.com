KSB Cast Steel Globe Valve

KSB Cast Steel Globe Valves are designed for precise regulation and throttling of fluid flow in pipelines. These valves use a disc and seat arrangement that allows accurate control over flow rate and pressure.

Constructed from high-quality cast steel, these valves are ideal for high pressure and high temperature applications, ensuring durability and reliable performance in demanding industrial environments.

Key Features
  • Designed for precise flow control and throttling
  • Cast steel construction for strength and durability
  • Bolted bonnet or pressure seal bonnet design
  • Rising or non-rising stem configurations
  • Flanged or butt weld end connections
  • Renewable seat and disc for extended service life
  • Available in various sizes and pressure ratings

Applications

  • Power generation plants
  • Oil and gas industries
  • Chemical and petrochemical industries
  • Water treatment systems
  • Steam and fluid control systems
  • Industrial process applications
