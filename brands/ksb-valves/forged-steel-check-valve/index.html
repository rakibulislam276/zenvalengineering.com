KSB Forged Steel Check Valve

KSB Forged Steel Check Valves are designed to prevent reverse flow in pipelines, ensuring safe and efficient operation of fluid systems. These valves automatically allow flow in one direction and close when the flow reverses.

Manufactured using forged steel, these valves offer superior strength, durability, and resistance to high pressure and temperature, making them ideal for critical industrial applications.

Key Features
  • Forged steel construction for high strength and durability
  • Prevents backflow and protects system integrity
  • Available in swing or lift type designs
  • Bolted bonnet or pressure seal bonnet options
  • Flanged, threaded, or butt weld end connections
  • Renewable seat and disc for longer service life
  • Suitable for high pressure and temperature applications

Applications

  • Oil and gas industries
  • Petrochemical plants
  • Power generation units
  • Chemical processing industries
  • High pressure steam systems
  • Industrial process pipelines
