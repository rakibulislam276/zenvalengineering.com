KSB Forged Steel Gate Valve

KSB Forged Steel Gate Valves are designed for reliable on/off isolation service in high-pressure and high-temperature applications. These valves use a wedge-shaped gate that moves perpendicular to the flow, ensuring tight shut-off and minimal pressure loss.

Manufactured from forged steel, these valves provide superior strength, durability, and resistance to extreme operating conditions, making them suitable for critical industrial systems.

Key Features
  • Designed for isolation (on/off) service
  • Forged steel construction for enhanced strength
  • Rising or non-rising stem design options
  • Bolted bonnet or pressure seal bonnet configurations
  • Flanged, threaded, or butt weld end connections
  • Renewable seat and disc for extended service life
  • Suitable for high pressure and temperature applications

Applications

  • Oil and gas industries
  • Petrochemical plants
  • Power generation units
  • Chemical processing industries
  • High pressure steam systems
  • Industrial process pipelines
