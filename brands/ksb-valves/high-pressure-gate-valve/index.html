KSB High Pressure Gate Valve

KSB High Pressure Gate Valves are designed to handle elevated pressure and temperature conditions in demanding industrial applications. These valves provide reliable isolation by using a wedge-shaped gate that ensures tight shut-off and minimal pressure loss.

Built for performance and durability, these valves are suitable for critical services where high pressure operation and long service life are essential.

Key Features
  • Designed for high pressure and high temperature applications
  • Provides tight shut-off with minimal leakage
  • Robust forged or cast steel construction
  • Rising or non-rising stem design options
  • Pressure seal or bolted bonnet configurations
  • Flanged or butt weld end connections
  • Long service life with low maintenance

Applications

  • Power generation plants
  • Oil and gas transmission systems
  • Petrochemical industries
  • Chemical processing plants
  • High pressure steam systems
  • Industrial process pipelines
