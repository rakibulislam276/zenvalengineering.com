KSB Cast Check Valve

KSB Cast Check Valves are designed to allow fluid flow in one direction while preventing reverse flow in pipelines. These valves are widely used in industrial applications to ensure safe and efficient operation of fluid systems.

Manufactured using high-quality cast materials, these valves provide excellent durability, reliable sealing, and long service life under varying pressure and temperature conditions.

Key Features
  • Allows one-way flow and prevents backflow
  • Robust cast construction for strength and durability
  • Available in swing or lift type designs
  • Metal-to-metal seating for tight shut-off
  • Flanged or threaded end connections
  • Available in various sizes and pressure ratings
  • Reliable performance with minimal maintenance

Applications

  • Water supply and distribution systems
  • Wastewater treatment plants
  • Oil and gas industries
  • Chemical processing plants
  • Power generation units
  • Industrial fluid handling systems
