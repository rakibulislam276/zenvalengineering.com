KSB Cast Steel Gate Valve

KSB Cast Steel Gate Valves are designed for reliable on/off isolation service in pipelines. These valves use a wedge-shaped gate that moves perpendicular to the flow, providing tight shut-off and minimal pressure drop.

Manufactured from high-quality cast steel, these valves are suitable for handling high pressure and temperature conditions, making them ideal for demanding industrial applications.

Key Features
  • Designed for isolation (on/off) service
  • Cast steel construction for high strength and durability
  • Rising or non-rising stem design options
  • Bolted bonnet or pressure seal bonnet design
  • Flanged or butt weld end connections
  • Renewable seat and disc for extended service life
  • Available in various sizes and pressure ratings

Applications

  • Oil and gas industries
  • Power generation plants
  • Chemical processing industries
  • Water treatment systems
  • Steam and fluid pipelines
  • Industrial process systems
