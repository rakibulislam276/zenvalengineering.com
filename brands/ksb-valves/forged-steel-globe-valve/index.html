KSB Forged Steel Globe Valve

KSB Forged Steel Globe Valves are designed for precise regulation and throttling of fluid flow in pipelines. These valves provide accurate control by adjusting the position of the disc, ensuring stable and efficient flow management.

Manufactured from forged steel, these valves offer superior strength, durability, and resistance to high pressure and temperature, making them ideal for critical and demanding industrial applications.

Key Features
  • Designed for precise flow control and throttling
  • Forged steel construction for enhanced strength and durability
  • Rising or non-rising stem configurations
  • Bolted bonnet or pressure seal bonnet design
  • Flanged, threaded, or butt weld end connections
  • Renewable seat and disc for longer service life
  • Suitable for high pressure and temperature applications

Applications

  • Oil and gas industries
  • Petrochemical plants
  • Power generation plants
  • Chemical processing industries
  • Steam and fluid control systems
  • Industrial process pipelines
