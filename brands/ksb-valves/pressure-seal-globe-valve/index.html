KSB Pressure Seal Globe Valve

KSB Pressure Seal Globe Valves are designed for precise flow control in high-pressure and high-temperature applications. These valves utilize a pressure seal bonnet design that enhances sealing integrity under extreme operating conditions.

Built for reliability and durability, these valves are widely used in critical industries where performance and safety are essential.

Key Features
  • Pressure seal bonnet design for high-pressure applications
  • Precise flow control and throttling capability
  • Suitable for high temperature services
  • Robust forged or cast steel construction
  • Flanged or butt weld end connections
  • Renewable seat and disc for extended service life
  • Reliable performance with minimal maintenance

Applications

  • Power generation plants
  • Oil and gas industries
  • Petrochemical plants
  • Chemical processing industries
  • High pressure steam systems
  • Industrial process pipelines
