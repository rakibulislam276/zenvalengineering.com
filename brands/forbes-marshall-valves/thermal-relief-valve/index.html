Thermal Relief Valve

Forbes Marshall Thermal Relief Valve Dealer, Supplier and Exporter in India

Thermal relief valves, also known as temperature relief valves or pressure-temperature relief valves, are safety devices designed to protect equipment and pipelines from excessive pressure caused by thermal expansion.

These valves automatically release pressure when it exceeds a preset limit, preventing damage to pipelines, pressure vessels, heat exchangers, and other industrial systems.

Key Features & Functions

  • Overpressure Protection: Protects systems by releasing excess pressure when the set limit is exceeded.
  • Temperature Response: Designed to respond to temperature-related pressure increases.
  • Automatic Operation: Opens automatically when pressure or temperature reaches the preset level.
  • Spring Loaded Mechanism: Uses a spring-loaded system to maintain valve closure until activation.
  • Safe Venting: Releases pressure safely to atmosphere or a designated relief line.
  • Versatile Applications: Suitable for both liquid and gas systems.
  • Code Compliance: Designed to comply with relevant industrial safety standards and codes.
