Single Orifice Float Trap SOFT

Single Orifice Float Trap SOFT Dealer in India

Single Orifice Float Trap (SOFT) is a steam trap designed for efficient removal of condensate and non-condensable gases from steam systems while preventing the loss of live steam.

The “Single Orifice” design means the trap contains one discharge orifice that allows condensate to drain while maintaining steam system efficiency.

Key Features & Characteristics

  • Single Orifice Design: Uses a single discharge orifice for efficient condensate removal.
  • Float Mechanism: A ball float responds to condensate levels and opens the discharge orifice automatically.
  • Continuous Operation: Operates continuously, discharging condensate as it forms.
  • Air Venting Capability: Efficiently removes air and non-condensable gases from the steam system.
  • Versatile Applications: Suitable for steam systems with varying loads and pressures.
  • Durable & Low Maintenance: Designed for long service life with minimal maintenance requirements.
