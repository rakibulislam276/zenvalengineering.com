Steam Pressure Reducing Valves FMPRV

Forbes Marshall Steam Pressure Reducing Valves FMPRV Dealer in India

Steam Pressure Reducing Valves (PRVs) are essential components in steam systems that control and maintain a predetermined downstream pressure. These valves ensure that high-pressure steam from the supply line is reduced to a safe and controlled pressure for downstream equipment and processes.

The Forbes Marshall FMPRV series is designed to provide reliable pressure control in industrial steam systems, helping improve system efficiency and protect equipment from excessive pressure.

Key Features & Functions

  • Pressure Reduction: Reduces high inlet steam pressure to a lower controlled outlet pressure.
  • Automatic Operation: Adjusts valve position automatically in response to upstream pressure variations.
  • Maintains Stable Pressure: Ensures consistent downstream pressure for stable system performance.
  • Industrial Applications: Widely used in power plants, industrial processes, and HVAC steam systems.
  • High Temperature Performance: Built with materials suitable for high-pressure and high-temperature steam conditions.
