Piston valves are industrial valves that use a piston mechanism to regulate the flow of fluids within a pipeline. These valves are known for their reliable performance, tight shut-off, and suitability for high-pressure and high-temperature applications.

Piston valves are widely used in steam systems and industrial fluid control applications where dependable sealing and durability are essential.

Key Features & Characteristics

  • Operating Principle: The valve uses a piston that moves vertically within a cylinder to control the opening and closing of the valve.
  • Tight Shut-Off: Provides excellent sealing performance and prevents leakage when fully closed.
  • Linear Flow Control: Allows precise control of flow rate and positioning between open and closed states.
  • Robust Construction: Designed to withstand high pressure and temperature conditions in demanding environments.
  • Industrial Applications: Widely used in power plants, steam systems, petrochemical industries, and process plants.
  • Materials of Construction: Typically manufactured using cast iron, carbon steel, stainless steel, or alloy steel depending on application requirements.
  • Types of Piston Valves: Includes sliding piston valves and lift-type piston valves designed for different operational needs.
  • Ease of Maintenance: Simple internal design allows easy inspection and maintenance.
