Thermodynamic Steam Trap FMTD

Forbes Marshall Thermodynamic Steam Trap FMTD Dealer in India

A thermodynamic steam trap is a steam trap designed to efficiently remove condensate from steam systems while preventing the escape of live steam. These traps ensure proper drainage of condensate and help maintain steam system efficiency.

Thermodynamic traps operate using the thermodynamic properties of steam and condensate, making them suitable for high-pressure steam applications.

Key Features & Characteristics

  • Operating Principle: Operates based on fluid dynamics and thermodynamic properties of steam and condensate.
  • Disc Mechanism: Uses a disc valve that responds to pressure and flow changes.
  • Dynamic Response: Opens to discharge condensate and closes to prevent steam loss.
  • High Pressure Capability: Suitable for high-pressure steam systems.
  • Compact Design: Small and simple design allows easy installation.
  • Durable Construction: Designed to withstand harsh industrial steam environments.
  • Wide Industrial Applications: Used in industrial processes and steam heating systems.
  • Low Maintenance: Simple design results in minimal maintenance requirements.
