Two Orifice Float Trap TOFT

Forbes Marshall Two Orifice Float Trap TOFT Dealer in India

The term TOFT stands for Two Orifice Float Trap. This steam trap is a mechanical device designed to discharge condensate, air, and other non-condensable gases from steam systems while preventing the loss of live steam.

The two-orifice design enables efficient removal of both condensate and air, ensuring reliable steam system performance and improved heat transfer efficiency.

Key Features & Functions

  • Float Mechanism: Contains a float that rises and falls with condensate level to open or close the discharge orifice.
  • Two Orifice Design: One orifice discharges condensate while the second removes air and non-condensable gases.
  • Air Venting: Prevents accumulation of air within the steam system for improved heat transfer efficiency.
  • Continuous Operation: Operates continuously, discharging condensate as it forms.
  • Suitable for Various Applications: Ideal for steam systems with varying loads and pressures.
  • Low Maintenance: Durable design requiring minimal maintenance.
  • Energy Efficient: Efficient condensate removal helps maintain optimal steam temperature and energy efficiency.
