Compact Module Two Orifice Float Trap (CMTOFT) is designed for efficient removal of condensate and non-condensable gases in steam systems. It combines the proven two-orifice float trap technology with a compact modular design for space-efficient installations.

These traps ensure continuous condensate discharge while maintaining steam system efficiency and preventing energy losses.

Key Features & Characteristics

  • Two Orifice Design: Separate orifices for condensate discharge and air removal ensure efficient operation.
  • Float Mechanism: A float responds to condensate level changes to control discharge automatically.
  • Continuous Operation: Operates continuously to remove condensate as it forms in steam systems.
  • Compact Module Design: Space-saving modular design suitable for modern industrial installations.
  • Versatile Applications: Suitable for various steam system applications where efficient condensate removal is required.
  • Low Maintenance: Durable construction ensures long service life with minimal maintenance.
