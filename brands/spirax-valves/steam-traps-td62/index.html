Spirax Sarco Steam Trap TD62

Spirax Sarco Steam Trap TD62 Dealer and Supplier in India

The Spirax Sarco Steam Trap TD62 is a thermodynamic steam trap designed for efficient removal of condensate and prevention of steam loss in industrial steam systems. These traps are widely used due to their compact design, durability, and reliable operation under varying pressure conditions.

Thermodynamic steam traps like the TD62 operate based on the principles of fluid dynamics and thermodynamics, making them suitable for a wide range of steam applications.

Key Features & Characteristics

  • Thermodynamic Operation: Operates using pressure and velocity differences between steam and condensate.
  • Disc Type Mechanism: Uses a disc to control opening and closing of the trap.
  • Efficient Condensate Removal: Ensures proper drainage while preventing steam loss.
  • Compact and Robust Design: Suitable for installation in limited spaces.
  • Durable Construction: Designed to withstand high pressure and temperature conditions.
  • Low Maintenance: Simple internal design reduces maintenance requirements.
  • Wide Pressure Range: Suitable for varying steam pressure conditions.
Applications
  • Steam Distribution Systems
  • Boiler Systems
  • Process Industries
  • Power Plants
  • Steam Heating Applications
  • Chemical Processing Plants
