Spirax Sarco Ball Float Steam Trap FT20

Spirax Sarco Ball Float Steam Trap FT20 Dealer and Supplier in India

The Spirax Sarco Ball Float Steam Trap FT20 is designed for continuous removal of condensate from steam systems while preventing the loss of live steam. It uses a ball float mechanism that automatically responds to condensate levels, ensuring efficient system operation.

Ball float steam traps are widely used in industrial applications where continuous drainage and reliable performance are required under varying load conditions.

Key Features & Characteristics

  • Ball Float Mechanism: Automatically opens and closes based on condensate levels.
  • Continuous Operation: Provides uninterrupted removal of condensate.
  • Modulating Discharge: Adjusts discharge rate according to condensate load.
  • Durable Construction: Suitable for high-pressure and high-temperature steam applications.
  • Automatic Air Venting: Efficiently removes air and non-condensable gases.
  • Easy Maintenance: Designed for quick inspection and servicing.
  • Reliable Performance: Ensures efficient and consistent steam system operation.
Applications
  • Steam Heating Systems
  • Industrial Process Plants
  • Power Generation Plants
  • Boiler Systems
  • Chemical Processing Industries
  • Steam Distribution Networks
