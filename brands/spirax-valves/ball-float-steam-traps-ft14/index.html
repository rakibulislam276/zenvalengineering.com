Spirax Sarco Ball Float Steam Trap FT14

Spirax Sarco Ball Float Steam Trap FT14 Dealer and Supplier in India

The Spirax Sarco Ball Float Steam Trap FT14 is designed for continuous removal of condensate from steam systems while preventing the loss of live steam. It uses a ball float mechanism that responds to condensate levels to ensure efficient discharge.

Ball float steam traps are widely used in steam systems due to their ability to provide continuous drainage, making them ideal for applications with varying condensate loads.

Key Features & Characteristics

  • Ball Float Mechanism: Automatically opens as condensate level rises, ensuring efficient discharge.
  • Continuous Operation: Provides continuous removal of condensate without interruption.
  • Modulating Discharge: Adjusts discharge rate based on condensate load.
  • Durable Construction: Designed for high-pressure and high-temperature steam systems.
