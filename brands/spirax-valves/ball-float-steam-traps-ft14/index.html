Spirax Sarco Ball Float Steam Trap FT14

Spirax Sarco Ball Float Steam Trap FT14 Dealer and Supplier in India

The Spirax Sarco Ball Float Steam Trap FT14 is a high-performance steam trap designed for continuous removal of condensate while preventing the loss of live steam. It uses a ball float mechanism that responds to condensate levels within the system.

Steam traps are essential components in steam systems, ensuring efficient heat transfer, energy savings, and proper system operation by removing condensate and air.

Key Features & Characteristics

  • Ball Float Mechanism: Automatically opens as condensate level rises, allowing efficient discharge.
  • Continuous Operation: Provides continuous condensate removal without interruption.
  • Modulating Discharge: Adjusts discharge rate based on condensate load.
  • Durable Construction: Built for high-pressure and high-temperature steam systems.
  • Automatic Air Venting: Removes air and non-condensable gases effectively.
  • Easy Maintenance: Designed for easy inspection and servicing.
  • Reliable Performance: Ensures optimal efficiency and protection of steam systems.
