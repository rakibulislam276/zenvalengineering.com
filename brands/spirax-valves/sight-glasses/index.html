Spirax Sarco Sight Glasses

Spirax Sarco Sight Glass Dealer and Supplier in India

Spirax Sarco Sight Glasses, also known as sight flow indicators or sight windows, are devices installed in pipelines or vessels to visually observe the flow or level of fluids. These devices allow operators to monitor the presence, clarity, and movement of liquids or steam condensate within the system.

Sight glasses are widely used in steam systems, chemical processing, water treatment plants, and other industrial processes where visual inspection of fluid flow is required.

Key Features & Characteristics

  • Transparent Viewing Window: Allows visual monitoring of fluid flow within pipelines.
  • Borosilicate Glass Construction: High-quality glass offers durability and resistance to thermal shock.
  • Multiple Sizes and Designs: Available in various sizes to suit different pipeline requirements.
  • Various Connection Types: Available with threaded, flanged, or welded connections.
  • Pressure and Temperature Resistant: Designed to operate in demanding industrial environments.
  • Easy Maintenance: Designed for easy cleaning, inspection, and replacement of glass components.
