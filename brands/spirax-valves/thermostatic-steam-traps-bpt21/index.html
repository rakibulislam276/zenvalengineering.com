Spirax Sarco Thermostatic Steam Trap BPT21

Spirax Sarco Thermostatic Steam Trap BPT21 Dealer and Supplier in India

The Spirax Sarco Thermostatic Steam Trap BPT21 is designed to remove condensate from steam systems using temperature-based operation. Thermostatic steam traps work by sensing the temperature difference between steam and condensate, allowing efficient discharge while preventing steam loss.

These traps are widely used in applications where precise temperature control and efficient condensate removal are required.

Key Features & Characteristics

  • Thermostatic Operation: Opens and closes based on temperature difference between steam and condensate.
  • Modulating Discharge: Adjusts discharge according to condensate load.
  • Automatic Operation: Responds automatically to system temperature and pressure changes.
  • Continuous Operation: Ensures efficient condensate removal without manual intervention.
  • Durable Construction: Built for high-pressure steam applications with long service life.
  • Easy Maintenance: Designed for quick inspection and servicing.
  • Reliable Performance: Maintains efficiency in steam systems.
Applications
  • Steam Heating Systems
  • Heat Exchangers
  • Process Industries
  • Boiler Systems
  • Power Plants
  • Chemical Processing Plants
