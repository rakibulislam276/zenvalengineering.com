Spirax Sarco Thermodynamic Steam Trap TD3

Spirax Sarco Thermodynamic Steam Trap TD3 Dealer and Supplier in India

The Spirax Sarco Thermodynamic Steam Trap TD3 is designed to efficiently remove condensate from steam systems while preventing the escape of live steam. Thermodynamic steam traps are widely used in industrial steam systems due to their reliability, compact design, and durability.

These traps operate using the thermodynamic properties of steam and condensate, ensuring effective condensate discharge while maintaining energy efficiency in steam networks.

Key Features & Characteristics

  • Thermodynamic Operation: Operates based on fluid dynamics and thermodynamic principles of steam and condensate.
  • Disc Mechanism: Uses a disc valve that opens and closes based on pressure and flow conditions.
  • Dynamic Response: Responds quickly to changing steam and condensate conditions.
  • Compact Design: Small and simple design allows easy installation in pipelines.
  • Durable Construction: Built to withstand high temperature and high pressure steam systems.
  • Low Maintenance: Simple internal mechanism reduces maintenance requirements.
