The Spirax Sarco Y Type Strainer is a filtration device commonly installed in pipelines to remove solid particles, debris, rust, and scale from fluid flow. These strainers help protect downstream equipment such as valves, pumps, and steam traps.

Spirax Sarco is a leading provider of steam system solutions and offers a wide range of industrial strainers designed for reliable performance in demanding process environments.

Y-type strainers are widely used in steam, water, gas, and oil pipelines to ensure smooth system operation and prevent damage to critical equipment.

Key Features & Functions

  • Efficient Filtration: Removes solid particles, rust, scale, and debris from pipelines.
  • Protection of Equipment: Prevents contaminants from damaging pumps, valves, and steam traps.
  • Y-Shaped Body Design: Allows efficient debris collection without significantly restricting flow.
