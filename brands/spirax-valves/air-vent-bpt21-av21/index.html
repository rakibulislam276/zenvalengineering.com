Spirax Sarco Air Vent BPT21 / AV21

Spirax Sarco Air Vent BPT21 / AV21 Dealer and Supplier in India

The Spirax Sarco Air Vent BPT21 / AV21 is designed to automatically remove air and non-condensable gases from steam systems. The presence of air in steam lines can reduce heat transfer efficiency and lead to operational issues such as water hammer and poor system performance.

Air vents play a critical role in maintaining system efficiency by ensuring that only pure steam is present in the pipeline.

Key Features & Characteristics

  • Air Removal: Efficiently removes trapped air and non-condensable gases.
  • Automatic Operation: Operates automatically without manual intervention.
  • Prevents Air Binding: Eliminates issues like reduced heat transfer and system inefficiency.
  • Compact Design: Easy to install in steam pipelines.
  • Durable Construction: Built using materials suitable for steam applications.
  • Wide Application Range: Suitable for various steam system installations.
