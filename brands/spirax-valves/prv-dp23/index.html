The Spirax Sarco PRV DP23 is a pressure reducing valve designed for precise control of steam pressure in industrial steam systems. Pressure reducing valves are essential components that regulate high steam pressure and maintain a stable downstream pressure.

Spirax Sarco is a globally recognized manufacturer of steam system solutions including pressure reducing valves, steam traps, strainers, and control valves used across various industries.

The DP23 model is commonly used in steam distribution systems where consistent pressure control is required for efficient and safe process operation.

Key Features & Functions

  • Pressure Reduction: Reduces high inlet steam pressure to a controlled downstream pressure.
  • Adjustable Set Pressure: Allows operators to adjust the pressure setpoint based on system requirements.
  • Automatic Operation: Responds automatically to pressure fluctuations in the system.
  • Safety Protection: Protects downstream equipment from excessive pressure.
  • Durable Construction: Manufactured using materials suitable for high temperature steam environments.
  • Wide Industrial Applications: Used in steam systems, power plants, and industrial process applications.
